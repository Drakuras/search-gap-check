Creator Search Insights workflow

Search Gap Saturation Check

Creator Search Insights shows demand. It doesn't show how much content already exists. Use this flow to check supply by hand in about a minute per term.

The flow

1
Creator Search Insights

Find a term with rising demand

Search a brand or niche. Look for a growing % and a "secret / hack / what they don't tell you" angle. Put the popularity and % into the demand checker below. It flags topic clusters (TikTok grouping lots of searches together) so you don't chase fake demand.

Rising %Content gap tabInsider angle
2
Copy

Copy the exact search phrase

Use the wording people type, not your own version of it.

3
TikTok search → Videos tab

Look at the top 10–15 videos only

That's what a searcher actually sees. Results are personalised, so treat it as a sample.

4
Score 4 signals

Engagement, recency, answer quality, competition

Add up the points (table below), or tap through the scorer.

5
Decide

Go, test, find a better angle, or skip

Then make content that answers the exact search. Re-check the term a few days later: if new videos jump, the window is closing.
